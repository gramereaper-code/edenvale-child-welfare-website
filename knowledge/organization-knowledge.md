## What the organisation does

Child Welfare SA Edenvale focuses on child protection, care and wellbeing, and assists families with children who need social services. Its public website describes:

- **Investigations of child abuse and neglect:** reported concerns are investigated; counselling may be offered; in severe cases, children may be placed in alternative care.
- **Foster care:** screening and guidance for prospective foster parents, with placement supervised by a social worker where applicable.
- **Child and family counselling:** support for troubled relationships, developmental, adjustment and personal challenges.
- **Family reconstruction:** support for biological parents when children have been placed in alternative care, following a detailed process.
- **Crisis intervention:** responding to immediate risk and connecting families to appropriate next steps.
- **Short-term practical support:** after a social-worker assessment, families in need may receive short-term food, clothing or subsidised preschool day-care support where applicable.
- **School counselling and mediation:** available in Edenvale and Bedfordview schools when requested.
- **Outreach:** parenting skills, child-development workshops, counselling, mediation, practical referrals and food-parcel support.

Do not promise eligibility, placement, food parcels, counselling or foster care. Explain that availability and assessment must be confirmed by the office.

## Pinocchio Educare Centre

Pinocchio Educare Centre is an early-childhood-development programme for preschool-age children from low-income families. The public website describes learning through play and stimulation, qualified staff, supervised care, snacks and a balanced cooked meal.

- The published programme is designed for children aged three to six to support school readiness.
- The public site says the centre accommodates up to 50 children.
- It describes playtime as supporting gross-motor development, basic concepts and socialisation.
- Families interested in current applications, places, eligibility, fees or opening dates must contact the main office. Do not promise a place.

## Community Empowerment Project

The public website describes objectives including individual self-development, developmental skills for children, parents and the community, emotional, social and physical wellbeing, collaboration with donors and NGOs, child protection, and a safe, healthy, secure place of care.

## How people can support the organisation

- **Donate or partner:** email administrator@edenvalechildwelfare.co.za or call 011 452 5940 to discuss current needs.
- **Wish list:** a public wish-list PDF is available on the website, but it was last updated in August 2022; advise visitors to contact the office for current needs.
